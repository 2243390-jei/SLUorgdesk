Step-by-Step Guide to Submitting Your Post-Event Report

Follow these simple steps to ensure your event is properly documented and compliant with SLU guidelines.

Go to the “Submit” page on the OrgDesk website.

Fill out all required fields in the submission form, including organization and applicant details.

Upload your Strategic Plans, Annual Report, CBL, and updated infographics.

Add your publication/event video link, ensuring access for osas@slu.edu.ph.

Review and submit your form. Wait for confirmation and monitor your dashboard for updates.
